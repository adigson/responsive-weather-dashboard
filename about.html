ABOUT THE PROJECT

Weather insights, wherever you are.

The Responsive Weather Dashboard makes it easy to check current conditions and see what the hours and days ahead may bring. Search for a city or use your device’s location to get started.

Explore the dashboard

What you can do

Find a place

Search for locations around the world or request weather for your current location.

See the full picture

Check temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, and precipitation.

Plan ahead

Browse an hourly forecast and a seven-day outlook, with metric and imperial units.

Use it anywhere

The responsive layout adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

Weather data and technology

Forecasts and place search are powered by the Open-Meteo APIs. The dashboard is built with semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, and uses browser geolocation only when you choose to locate yourself.
